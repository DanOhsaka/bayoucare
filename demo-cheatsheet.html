If they ask

"Is this real data?" — "Mixed, and we label which is which. The parish numbers are real: CDC PLACES 2025 for screening coverage and population, State Cancer Profiles / USCS for incidence and late stage — and where a parish had under 16 cases we show suppressed rather than an estimate. Everything else — patient records, risk models, payer packets — is synthetic, and every model is visible on screen. TrialMatch pulls live from ClinicalTrials.gov."

"Are these validated clinical models?" — "The lung risk figure is a labelled approximation of PLCOm2012 over the four inputs the app collects; the breast card is family-history criteria, not Tyrer-Cuzick. Both say so on screen. The counterfactual model is a transparent logistic regression with published weights — you can read it. We'd rather show our work than imply a validation we can't demonstrate."

"What does Ochsner actually get?" — "Two hours of clinician admin time back per week, earlier intervention on flagged patients, prior-auth packets drafted from the chart, and a parish heat index that directs outreach spend — plus a no-show model that protects the infusion chairs."

"How is this different from a national app?" — "They remind; we predict. They build for broadband cities; we build for Vermilion Parish. Offline-first, SMS fallback, caregiver-inclusive — and four languages, including Creole and Vietnamese."

"What's the pilot?" — "Two Ochsner oncology clinics, 50 patients, Q1 2027 — measuring treatment-start delay, check-in adherence, and admin time against ASCO quality measures."
