Understand — your report, in plain words

ASCO-aligned educationAI-assisted · not medical advice
DarleneI got my biopsy report and I'm scared. What does "ER+/PR+, HER2−" mean for me?
BayouCareThat's completely understandable — let's take it one line at a time. ER+/PR+ means your tumor uses estrogen/progesterone to grow, and HER2− means it doesn't use that protein. Good news: this type of breast cancer has many effective treatments, and hormone therapy can target it directly. I've simplified each section of your report below.
Pathology summary — in plain English
Invasive ductal carcinoma, Grade 2. A cancer that started in the milk duct and is growing slowly-to-moderately. Grade 2 means the cells look fairly similar to normal cells. This is the most common type of breast cancer and is very treatable, especially when caught early — yours was caught at Stage II, before it spread beyond the breast and nearby nodes.
Receptor status: ER+ / PR+ / HER2−
Think of it as the tumor's "remote control." ER+ and PR+ mean hormones in your body can "turn on" the cancer — so we can use medicines that block those hormones. HER2− means this type doesn't respond to HER2-targeted drugs, which is fine — other therapies work very well. Together, this profile responds well to hormone therapy + chemo, which is exactly the plan Dr. Peters built with you.
What to ask at your next visit
✅ How will we know the chemo is working?
✅ What side effects should I call about vs. manage at home?
✅ Should I take a genetic test (like BRCA) — and what would it mean for my daughters?
✅ What does "survivorship" look like for me after treatment ends?

BayouCare simplifies reports for understanding and always shows what to confirm with your care team. It never replaces clinical advice.

📊 Data & insights — official challenge partner: ASCO
American Society of Clinical Oncology

    
ASCO is the challenge's official data source. BayouCare operationalizes it in three ways: 1) screening schedules follow ASCO & ACS guidelines (Screen & Prevent module) · 2) patient education maps to ASCO's plain-language standards (Understand module) · 3) pilot outcomes benchmark against ASCO quality measures — time-to-treatment, surveillance adherence, and treatment-interruption flags (Care Team dashboard).
